Capilano Drive is a short street on the hill behind Marin Country Club, at the southern end of Novato. It is quiet in the way that only a road going nowhere in particular can be — and it is four minutes from the freeway.

01 — The setting

Ignacio is the part of Novato that most people pass through without registering: the stretch where Highway 101 runs between two sets of hills, with the bay flats opening to the east and the country club tucked into the western slope. The residential streets above the club — Capilano among them — were laid out to face outward, which is why a house here gets water, valley, and fairway from a single parcel.

What that buys, practically, is a rural-feeling address with an urban commute. The Ignacio Boulevard interchange is a few minutes down the hill. SMART rail runs the length of the county from stations north and south of here. The open space that hems in the valley on both sides is public and permanent, which keeps the ridgelines empty.

Novato itself sits at the top of Marin, far enough north to be materially less expensive than Ross or Mill Valley and close enough that the difference in drive time to the Golden Gate is measured in minutes rather than hours. The town has a working downtown, a large public school district, and more protected land per resident than anywhere else on the 101 corridor.

Distances and drive times below are approximate, measured from the Capilano Drive ridge, and offered for orientation only. Buyer to verify all figures, school assignment, and transit schedules independently.

02 — What is nearby

Marin Country Club

Adjacent

The fairways run directly below the ridge. The clubhouse and entrance are on Country Club Drive, a short drive around the base of the hill. Golf, tennis, pool, and dining; membership by application.

Highway 101 — Ignacio Boulevard

≈ 1.5 mi

The nearest full interchange, giving southbound access toward San Rafael and the Golden Gate and northbound toward Petaluma and Sonoma County.

SMART — Novato Hamilton station

≈ 3 mi

The closest rail stop to the property. Commuter service south to Larkspur, connecting to the Golden Gate Ferry for a car-free route into San Francisco.

SMART — Novato San Marin station

≈ 6 mi

The northern Novato stop, serving the San Marin and downtown side of town on the same Larkspur–Santa Rosa line.

Ignacio & Indian Valley open space

≈ 1–2 mi

Marin County Open Space District preserves on both sides of the valley, with trailheads minutes from the house and connections through to Indian Valley, Loma Verde, and the wider ridge network.

Novato Unified School District

District-wide

The property lies within Novato Unified. Elementary, middle, and high school assignment is set by district boundary and subject to change — confirm current assignment directly with the district.

Downtown Novato

≈ 5 mi

Grant Avenue: grocery, restaurants, the farmers' market, and the civic center, roughly ten minutes north on Redwood Boulevard or 101.

San Francisco

≈ 30 mi · 45 min

A straight run down 101 across the Golden Gate. Roughly forty minutes outside of peak hours; allow an hour or more inbound on weekday mornings. Larkspur ferry offers the alternative.

San Francisco International (SFO)

≈ 45 mi

About an hour without traffic. Oakland International is comparable by distance via the Richmond–San Rafael Bridge.
