Ignacio Valley · Novato · Marin County

Above the club, below the ridge line.

A short street on the hill behind Marin Country Club, quiet in the way only a road going nowhere can be — and four minutes from the freeway.

Marin Country Club

Down the hill, and open all year.

The fairways begin where the ridge ends. Eighteen holes laid out by Lawrence Hughes in 1957, rebuilt by John Harbottle III in 2007 and reworked again in 2017 — with four lighted tennis courts, a standing pickleball contingent, three pools, a fitness center, and a clubhouse that serves dinner.

Most of the county drives to a club like this one. From this ridge it is the bottom of the hill.
